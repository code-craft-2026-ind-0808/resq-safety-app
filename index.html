🛡️ Safety Tips

  • Stay calm during an emergency.
  • Move to a safe location if possible.
  • Contact emergency services when necessary.
  • Keep important emergency contacts saved.
  • Do not share personal information with strangers.

🏥 Basic First Aid

  • For minor cuts, clean the wound and cover it.
  • For burns, cool the area with clean running water.
  • If someone is unconscious, contact emergency services immediately.
  • Do not move seriously injured people unless necessary for safety.
